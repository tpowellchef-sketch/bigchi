The Foundation

Built on Resilience & Inclusion

At Big Chi Martial Arts, located right in the heart of Sheffield at 2 Joiner St, S3 8GW, we believe true strength is forged from the inside out. Our approach combines rigorous combat training with deep psychological support and holistic wellness.

With extensive background training in MMA, boxing, and kickboxing, combined with professional qualifications in therapy, we specialize in supporting individuals from challenged backgrounds and neurodivergent communities. We provide a safe, disciplined space where everyone can unlock their full potential.
